This is the technical glossary: on-chain accounts, PDA seeds, and CPI plumbing. For plain-language definitions of the same product concepts, see [the customer glossary](/reference/glossary).

## E

### Engine auth

A single program-derived address (`["engine_auth"]` under `perp_engine`) that owns every per-market vault and signs all CPI calls into `pool_program`, `oracle_adapter`, and `fee_distributor`. Centralising authority on one PDA means USDC custody, oracle reads, and fee splits all live behind the same on-chain signer.

Defined in the `perp_engine` program.

## M

### Market state

The per-market account that stores open-interest totals, funding accumulators, reserved liquidity, the payout queue head and tail, and configuration such as MMR and max leverage. There is one `MarketState` PDA per supported symbol.

Defined in the `perp_engine` program.

## O

### Oracle

The price-feed subsystem comprising the `oracle_adapter` program, per-market `PriceFeed` PDAs, and the off-chain price-relay service. Off-hours prices come from an off-exchange reference feed; the relayer writes the secondary PriceFeed PDA and oracle_adapter falls back to it when the primary ages out. Under the pyth-hybrid oracle, `oracle_adapter` reads Pyth's `PriceUpdateV2` as primary during RTH for the free-RTH markets and falls back to the relayer-written `PriceFeed` PDA off-hours; the four relayed-24/7 markets (MU, SNDK, INTC, AMD) read the `PriceFeed` PDA in every session. Reads therefore succeed around the clock. Every trader-facing instruction in `perp_engine` reads price via CPI into `oracle_adapter`, never by deserialising the feed directly. The on-chain price-deviation circuit breaker is disabled — bad-data rejection happens off-chain.

Defined in the `oracle_adapter` program.

### Open-interest accumulators

The per-market long and short open-interest totals (`long_oi` / `short_oi`) tracked on `MarketState`. Their imbalance drives the **funding rate**. In principle the same skew could also push the mark price away from the index, but mark-skew (price impact) is **disabled today**, so funding — not mark skew — is the active lever for an imbalanced book. If skew impact is ever enabled, this changes.

See: [Mark price (vs index)](/reference/glossary#mark-price-vs-index)

### Order PDA

A program-derived address holding a pending limit or stop order until the keeper triggers it. Order PDAs are seeded by `(owner, market_id, client_id)` so a trader can deterministically address their own orders without on-chain enumeration.

Defined in the `perp_engine` program.

## P

### PayoutQueueEntry

The on-chain record for a single queued payout. Fields include `owner`, `amount`, `enqueued_ts`, and a `voided` flag. Entries live in a per-market ring buffer addressable by `(market_id, seq)` so the keeper can claim them in order.

See: [Payout queue (customer glossary)](/reference/glossary#payout-queue)

### PriceFeed PDA

The on-chain account written by the off-chain price-relay service and read by `oracle_adapter`. Decode it via the SDK (`PriceFeedClient` / `utils/priceMath`); the binary layout is internal and not intended for hand parsing.

Defined in the `price_feed` program.

## R

### Reserve factor

A multiplier applied to the unrealised profit of open positions when computing reserved liquidity. A factor above 1.0 buffers the pool against rapid price moves between liquidity checkpoints.

See: [Reserved liquidity (customer glossary)](/reference/glossary#reserved-liquidity)

## S

### Side bucket

A single per-pool USDC accumulator (`pool.side_bucket`) that parks LP gains from losing closes while the payout queue is active, so the next `harvest` has funds to pay queued winners. It is **not** an open-interest accumulator. Losing closes credit it; harvests draw it down; once the queue fully drains it flushes into `total_usdc` and resets to 0. Bad debt eats the side bucket first (and only overflows onto `total_usdc` if the bucket is empty).

Defined in the `pool_program`.

See: [LP payout queue](/developer/lp-payout-queue#where-lp-gains-park-the-side-bucket), [Payout queue (customer glossary)](/reference/glossary#payout-queue)

## V

### Vault

A USDC token account owned by `engine_auth` that holds all trader collateral for a specific market. There is one vault PDA per market, and only `engine_auth` can sign transfers out of it via CPI through the SPL token program.

Defined in the `perp_engine` program.

### Voided entry

A `PayoutQueueEntry` whose `voided` flag has been set — typically because the underlying position was liquidated, modified, or otherwise resolved before the payout reached the queue head. Voided entries are skipped during drain without paying out, but still consume their slot in sequence order until the head pointer passes them.

See: [Payout queue (customer glossary)](/reference/glossary#payout-queue)
