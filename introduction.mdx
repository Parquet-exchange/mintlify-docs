Parquet is a decentralized perpetuals exchange on Solana for synthetic long/short exposure to US stocks and ETFs, with USDC collateral and on-chain oracle price feeds. **Markets trade 24/7.** During regular US trading hours (RTH) prices track the live US equity market. During off-hours the prices come from an off-exchange reference feed. Parquet offers tiered leverage up to **200×** (smallest position tier), applied **24/7** — the same tier table in regular hours and off-hours. 200× is the maximum you can open; at that cap a fresh position sits near its liquidation point once the open fee is taken, so high leverage is high risk. Maintenance margin is 40 bps venue-wide. Off-hours keeps a tighter open-interest cap and off-exchange reference pricing.

Trading is powered by per-market USDC liquidity pools, and every position is opened against its market's pool at the oracle index price. Stakers earn a share of protocol fee distributions, and liquidity providers earn the LP share of trading and borrowing fees — and gain when traders realize losses — by depositing into a market's pool on the [LP farm](/liquidity/overview).

Prices come from external market-data feeds, are aggregated off-chain, validated for staleness and confidence, and kept continuously up to date on-chain. During US regular trading hours (RTH) prices track the live US equity market; off-hours the feed switches to an off-exchange reference, with a tighter open-interest cap (the leverage tiers are now the same in every session). Because the off-hours reference keeps updating, the on-chain feed stays current around the clock. This allows trading to stay live 24/7. Feeds are external and can still go stale; see the [risk disclosure](/reference/risks).

## Markets

Parquet lists 32 active markets — 31 US equity perps (29 single-name equities plus the SPY and QQQ ETFs) and the SPCX synthetic (a SpaceX synthetic priced from a third-party Hyperliquid feed; not SpaceX stock). See [Markets](/markets) for the full listing, including the inactive symbols (GLD, SMCI, USO) recognized for historical reasons but with no live price feed.
