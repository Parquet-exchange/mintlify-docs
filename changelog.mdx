A summary of public-relevant changes to Parquet. This list is sanitized for traders and integrators — it omits internal infrastructure and operational detail. For the full risk picture, always read the [risk disclosure](/reference/risks); for the current numbers, see [Fees & Costs](/trading/fees).

## 2026-06-11

- **SPCX pre-IPO synthetic listed.** A 32nd market, SPCX, is now live — a SpaceX pre-IPO synthetic priced 24/7 from a third-party Hyperliquid feed. It is not SpaceX stock and has no Pyth feed. See [Markets](/markets) and the [risk disclosure](/reference/risks).
- **LP farm live.** The liquidity-provider farm is live on mainnet. See [Liquidity](/liquidity/overview).

## 2026-06-09

- **Market lineup expanded to 31 equity perps.** Sixteen markets were added (MSFT, META, PLTR reactivated plus ORCL, LLY, TSM, CRWV, BABA, AVGO, NFLX, MRVL, ASML, COST, RIVN, IBM, DELL), all relayed 24/7. GLD, SMCI, and USO remain recognized on-chain but inactive. See [Markets](/markets).
- **Liquidation fee moved to equity-based.** The liquidation fee is now **20% of remaining equity at liquidation**, capped at equity and split 50/50 between the liquidator and the insurance fund. It replaces the prior fixed notional fee. Because it scales with remaining equity, it cannot by itself create bad debt. See [Liquidations](/trade/liquidations).
- **Funding activated.** Funding now accrues continuously on every market from the open-interest imbalance, capped at **10 bps/hour**, and settles when a position is closed or modified. Positions held before this date had accrued no funding. See [Fees & Costs](/trading/fees).

## 2026-06-07

- **Protocol-token staking live.** Stake PARQ — the protocol's fixed-supply Token-2022 mint — to earn a claimable USDC share of protocol fee distributions, weighted by lockup tier (7 days 2×, 90 days 10×, 180 days 50×). See [Staking & Rewards](/staking/overview) and [$PARQ token](/staking/tokenomics).

## 2026-06-05

- **24/7 high-leverage unlock.** Off-hours leverage now matches regular hours — the same tier table applies in every session, **up to 200×** on the smallest position tier (set by the 50 bps initial margin). Off-hours keeps its tighter $500K open-interest cap and off-exchange reference pricing. See [Markets](/markets) and [Trading overview](/trading/overview).
- **ADL tail-backstop armed (off-hours).** A last-resort, bounded auto-deleveraging backstop now sits behind the [payout queue](/trade/payout-queue) on all markets off-hours: in an extreme, sustained imbalance it can apply a capped, pro-rata haircut to the oldest *unbacked* queued payouts so the tail cannot grow without bound. It never touches open positions or collateral and does not fire in normal operation. See [Risks](/reference/risks#adl-tail-backstop).

## 2026-06-03

- **Fee distribution finalized to 60 / 25 / 10 / 5.** Of every trading fee, **60%** now goes to LPs, **25%** to stakers, **10%** to the treasury, and **5%** to the referral reward pool. See [Fees & Costs](/trading/fees).
- **Referral Standard tier set.** Referrers earn **25%** of their referred traders' trading fees; referred traders get **10% off** their fees. Referral deeplinks now bind on a new trader's first trade. See [Referrals](/referrals).

## 2026-05-31

- **Feed-v2 15-market lineup live.** The active market set is now 15 symbols — 13 single-name US equities plus the SPY and QQQ ETFs. A few legacy symbols remain recognized on-chain but inactive. See [Markets](/markets).

## 2026-05-27

- **Mainnet launch.** Parquet went live on Solana mainnet with real USDC, 24/7 trading, tiered leverage, and continuous funding. See the [risk disclosure](/reference/risks) before trading.

## 2026-05-20

- **24/7 trading unlocked.** Markets stay open continuously. During US regular trading hours prices track the live equity market; off-hours pricing comes from an off-exchange reference feed under tighter risk parameters (lower leverage caps and a lower open-interest cap). See [Trading overview](/trading/overview).
